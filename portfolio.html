About

I'm a robotics software engineer based in Ulm, Germany, specializing in ROS2-based humanoid robotics, dexterous manipulation, and robot perception. Over the past two years I've helped build semi-humanoid robotic systems from the ground up — 5-finger dexterous hands, teleoperation interfaces, ROS2 middleware, SDKs, APIs, and the sensor integration frameworks that hold it all together.

My background spans the full robotics stack: motion planning with MoveIt!2, Gazebo and MuJoCo simulation, computer vision with OpenCV and YOLO, and simulation-to-real deployment on physical hardware. I like the parts other people skip — the SDK nobody wants to write, the ROS1-to-ROS2 migration nobody wants to own, the sensor pipeline that has to just work.

Currently finishing a Master's in Mechatronics, with a thesis built around one hard problem: getting a 5-finger humanoid gripper to sense, plan, and act like it means it.
