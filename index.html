About

I'm a Robotics Software Engineer based in Ulm, Germany, specializing in ROS2-based humanoid robotics, dexterous manipulation, and robotic system integration. Over the past two years, I've contributed to the development of semi-humanoid robotic systems, including 5-finger dexterous robotic hands, teleoperation interfaces, ROS2 middleware, SDKs, APIs, and multi-sensor integration frameworks.

My experience spans the full robotics software stack, from robot control, motion planning with MoveIt!2, simulation in Gazebo and MuJoCo, and computer vision with OpenCV and YOLO, to hardware communication, real-time sensor integration, and simulation-to-real deployment on physical robotic systems. I also have hands-on experience with industrial robotics and automation, working with KUKA and ABB robotic platforms, Siemens TIA Portal, PLC programming, and robot simulation.

I enjoy solving the engineering challenges that make robotic systems reliable, scalable, and ready for real-world deployment. My work includes ROS1-to-ROS2 migration, SDK and API development, hardware-software integration, sensor pipelines, system validation, and automated software testing. I hold a Master's degree in Mechatronics, where my thesis focused on designing and implementing a ROS-based control system for a 5-finger humanoid robotic gripper, integrating motion planning, teleoperation, tactile sensing, and real-world robotic interaction.
