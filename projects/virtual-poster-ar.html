PERSONAL PROJECT

Virtual Poster Positioning (AR)

Computer-vision-driven augmented reality — placing virtual posters accurately and stably into real-world scenes using marker-based tracking.

OpenCV ArUco Markers Camera Calibration Augmented Reality

Overview

An AR positioning system built around ArUco markers: detect the marker, estimate depth and pose, and lock a virtual poster convincingly into the real scene, frame to frame.

What I built

  • Leveraged OpenCV and ArUco markers for accurate real-world detection and tracking.
  • Enhanced the visual experience with realistic, dynamic poster positioning within AR environments.
  • Implemented camera calibration and depth estimation for stable, accurate AR interactions.
