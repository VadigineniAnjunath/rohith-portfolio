Assistive Mobility Robot

A vehicle fitted with a robotic arm for physically handicapped users, able to pick up and transport everyday items alongside autonomous navigation.

Arduino IDE C++ Assistive Technology Robotic Arm

Overview

Built to explore practical assistive robotics: a mobility platform paired with a simple pick-and-place arm, aimed at giving physically handicapped users a way to retrieve and move everyday items independently.

What I built

  • Engineered a mobility vehicle fitted with a robotic arm for physically handicapped users.
  • Implemented a robotic arm for picking up and transporting basic items, adding practical utility beyond navigation alone.
  • Developed the complete control system in C++ under the Arduino IDE for coordinated vehicle and arm movement.
